About

Browser remake of Win32 Sokoban, originally written in pure x86 assembly by Crow16384 in 2004 for his wife — 138 levels and unlimited undo.

Classic (1–50), Extra (51–92), and Yoshio Murase (93–138) packs are preserved from the original. Pixel sprites are the originals; the shell is new.
